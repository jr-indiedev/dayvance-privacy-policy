1. Introduction

Dayvance is a habit tracking and daily planning application designed to help users organize routines, build habits and monitor personal progress. Dayvance is designed around local data storage. This policy explains the information the app accesses, stores and uses on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, Dayvance may store the following information locally:

Habit and daily planning information

  • Habits and scheduled activities you create
  • Daily plans and habit completion records
  • Daily progress and routine information

Personal tracking information

  • Sleep and wake-up records
  • Water consumption records
  • Step-count records
  • Yoga activity and progress
  • Mood and other personal tracking records supported by the app

Screen-time information

Dayvance offers an optional screen-time feature. If you enable Android Usage Access, the app accesses Android usage statistics to calculate foreground usage time for X (formerly Twitter), YouTube and Reddit. The app calculates a combined daily total for these selected apps. This information is processed locally. The feature does not read messages, passwords or the contents of your activity within those apps. You can revoke Usage Access in your Android device settings.

Images and files

If you select images or files through app features, selected files may be stored in the app's private local storage and used for the relevant functionality.

3. Local data storage

Dayvance uses local device storage, including SharedPreferences and app-private storage, to maintain app information. The current implementation does not provide a Dayvance cloud database, account system or cloud synchronization for personal tracking information. The app's tracking features are intended to operate locally on your device.

4. No personal tracking data sharing by Dayvance

Dayvance does not intentionally transmit personal tracking information to the developer, external servers or third-party data-sharing services. Dayvance does not sell personal tracking information. This statement concerns the app's own tracking functionality; it does not cover information you choose to provide directly to an external service or information handled by your device platform.

5. User-controlled data export

Dayvance provides an option to export supported app information in JSON format. When you choose to export, the app can copy relevant data to your device's clipboard. You decide whether to share that exported information. If you voluntarily share an exported JSON file or copied information with another person or service, that sharing is initiated by you and is outside Dayvance's control. Clipboard contents may be accessible to other apps or services depending on your device and operating system.

6. Data deletion

Your information is stored locally on your device. You can use available in-app deletion options to remove supported tracking history. You can also clear Dayvance's app data through Android settings or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app information. Exported files, clipboard contents or information you independently shared outside Dayvance are not automatically deleted when you clear app data or uninstall the app.

7. App permissions

Usage Access

Used to calculate screen-time information for the selected apps described in this policy.

Photo and file selection

Used when you voluntarily select images or files through available app features.

You can manage applicable permissions through your Android device settings.

8. External links

Dayvance may provide links to external platforms such as Discord, Ko-fi and Trello. These platforms operate independently and their own privacy policies and terms apply when you visit or use them. Dayvance does not control information you independently provide to those services.

9. Children's privacy

Dayvance is a general habit tracking and personal planning application. The current app does not implement a user account system. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of mobile applications and device permissions.

10. Security

Dayvance stores personal tracking information locally on your device. The security of this information also depends on your device's security settings, operating system and physical access controls. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with an appropriate screen lock. No electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, data practices or applicable requirements change. Updated versions will be made available on this page. The effective date at the beginning of this document indicates when the latest version took effect.
